What is the Buteyko method?

The Buteyko method is a health system for normalizing breathing. It is aimed at reducing excessive ventilation of the lungs and forming a physiological breathing pattern. Breath holds are only one part of it: the method includes different levels of practice, relaxation and carrying calm breathing into everyday life.

The method is associated with the name of the Soviet scientist Konstantin Buteyko (1923-2003).

The breathing paradox

If you breathe excessively deeply and ventilate your lungs more than your body needs, this does not necessarily improve the delivery of oxygen to the tissues. The release of oxygen to the tissues is described, among other things, by the Bohr effect.

During the sessions you gradually form a calm breathing pattern: with relaxation and under an instructor’s supervision.

Control pause: why and how to measure it

The control pause is the time from a normal exhalation to the first desire to inhale. Every session starts with this figure: it and your pulse show how your breathing changes over time.

About three minutes: two minutes of rest and the measurement itself

  1. Sit quietly for two minutes.
  2. Breathe out normally, not deeply, and pinch your nose.
  3. Press “Start”, and at the first desire to inhale press “Stop”.
2:00rest before the measurement

Sit comfortably and start with two minutes of rest.

Does the Buteyko method replace medical treatment?

No. The Buteyko method does not replace medical diagnosis and treatment. An instructor does not prescribe or cancel medicines and does not change a doctor’s prescriptions. If you have a medical condition, first discuss the sessions with your treating doctor and continue taking everything the doctor has prescribed.

Can I study online from another country?

Yes, the training is fully online, so you can take part from any country. Sessions are held on Zoom, Telegram or another messenger that suits you, and you agree on a convenient time in advance personally with Alik. To pay with a foreign card, write to him and he will send you the payment details.

Do I need to be physically fit?

You can start without any special preparation. Sessions take place at home, physical exercise is not compulsory, and every session takes your condition into account. If your doctor has given you any restrictions, mention them in the first conversation so that the exercises and pace are chosen with these restrictions in mind from the very beginning.

What happens at the first session?

The first session begins with getting acquainted and assessing your initial condition. You measure your control pause and pulse, and then do your first practice under Alik’s supervision. This gives you a starting point. In later sessions, the pause, pulse, correctness of the exercises and how you feel are monitored.
