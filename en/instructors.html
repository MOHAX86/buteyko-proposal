Sample diploma of a VEDB instructor who has the right to teach other instructors.

That one of the key problems at the cellular level is an insufficient supply of oxygen to the tissues. And impaired oxygen supply is to a significant degree connected with the way we breathe.

In the photo: the outstanding Soviet scientist Konstantin Buteyko (1923 - 2003)

There is a breathing paradox that far from everyone knows about: if a person breathes excessively deeply and ventilates the lungs more than the body requires, this does not necessarily improve the delivery of oxygen to the tissues.

One of the physiological mechanisms connected with the release of oxygen from hemoglobin to the tissues is described by the Bohr effect.
