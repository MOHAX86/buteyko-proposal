A sample diploma of a VEDB senior instructor who has the right to teach other instructors.

One of the key problems at the cellular level is an insufficient supply of oxygen to the tissues. And a disturbance of oxygen supply is to a large extent connected with the features of breathing.

In the photo: the outstanding Soviet scientist Konstantin Buteyko (1923–2003)

There is a breathing paradox that not everyone knows about: if a person breathes excessively deeply and ventilates the lungs more than the body needs, this does not necessarily improve the delivery of oxygen to the tissues.

One of the physiological mechanisms connected with the release of oxygen from hemoglobin to the tissues is described by the Bohr effect.
