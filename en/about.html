About me

Welcome, friends!

I am glad to welcome you to my page. So that you can get to know me better, both as a person and as a specialist, I decided to devote this space to an account of myself, my family and my path in life.

My name is Alik Mullakhmetov, I am 55 years old. You can view my diplomas, certificates and academic profile below.

Throughout my work I have helped people not only to restore physical functions but also to feel once again a connection with their breathing, mindfulness and the body’s natural ability to recover. This project reflects my life’s work: to support health improvement that goes beyond ordinary treatment and sees a human being as a whole person.

After normalizing your breathing with the Buteyko method, you will be able to improve your health indicators, manage your condition better and reduce the manifestations of many problems that may have troubled you for years. You will gain a clear understanding of how to maintain healthy breathing, recovery and inner balance, and you will also gradually build useful habits that will become part of your everyday life.

Wishing you a good journey!

One of the key problems at the cellular level is an insufficient supply of oxygen to the tissues. And impaired oxygen supply is to a significant degree connected with the way we breathe.

In the photo: the outstanding Soviet scientist Konstantin Buteyko (1923–2003)

There is a breathing paradox that far from everyone knows about: if a person breathes excessively deeply and ventilates the lungs more than the body requires, this does not necessarily improve the delivery of oxygen to the tissues.

One of the physiological mechanisms connected with the release of oxygen from hemoglobin to the tissues is described by the Bohr effect.
