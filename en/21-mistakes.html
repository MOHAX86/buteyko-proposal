A familiar situation

You practice regularly, but your control pause does not grow

A person can practice for weeks and months without understanding why their readings do not improve. Most often the reason lies in details that are hard to notice on your own.

  • They breathe less oftenInstead of reducing the depth of breathing, they start reducing its frequency.
  • They stretch out breath holdsThey believe that the longer you endure, the better, and make breath holds the main exercise.
  • They tense upInstead of relaxing, they clamp the breathing muscles, chest and abdomen.
  • They measure the CP incorrectlyThey “squeeze out” the control pause by endurance or break into a deep inhalation.
  • They keep changing exercisesThey look for “the most effective one” and do not carry the basic training through to a result.

The book teaches you to find such mistakes, understand their causes and correct your practice in time.

Mistake No. 2 from the book

The whole method fits on the fingers of your left hand

K. P. Buteyko suggested memorizing the essence of the method on five fingers. Beginners often remember the exercises but forget the principle for which they are done.

  1. 1reducing
  2. 2the depth
  3. 3of breathing
  4. 4by relaxing the diaphragm
  5. 5to an air shortage

A check for any exercise. If you doubt whether you are breathing correctly, ask yourself three questions:

Am I reducing precisely the depth of breathing? Is this happening through relaxation? Does the necessary sensation of air shortage appear?

Important safety information

The book is intended for educational and informational purposes. It does not replace examination, monitoring and treatment by qualified specialists.

  • People with heart and vascular, respiratory, neurological and other chronic diseases, as well as pregnant women, need to talk to their treating doctor before practicing.
  • In case of dizziness, feeling faint or a noticeable worsening of how you feel, the exercise must be stopped.
  • In case of chest pain, pronounced difficulty breathing or loss of consciousness, you must call an ambulance immediately.
  • Breath holds must not be performed in water, while driving or in any situation where a possible loss of consciousness is dangerous.
  • Information from the book is not grounds for stopping medicines, changing dosages or refusing prescribed treatment.

The body’s response to breathing exercises is individual. The author does not guarantee any particular therapeutic or health results.
