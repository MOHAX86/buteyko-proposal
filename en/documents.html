Documents on the Buteyko method

Official documents from 1983–1990 on Konstantin Buteyko’s method of Voluntary Elimination of Deep Breathing (VEDB).

Important. These are historical documents. They do not replace medical advice and do not promise a result. Do not stop or change your medicines without a doctor.

Author’s certificate No. 1067640 “A method of treating hemohypocarbia”

Issued to Konstantin Pavlovich Buteyko. Priority of the invention: 29 January 1962. Entered in the State Register of Inventions of the USSR: 15 September 1983.

Claim of the invention

A method of treating hemohypocarbia, characterised in that, in order to shorten the course of treatment, for example of bronchial asthma, lung ventilation is reduced to a volume the patient can tolerate, the level of carbon dioxide in exhaled air is monitored, and the rate of its increase is kept at no more than 3–7 mmHg per day up to a level of 45–55 mmHg.

Author’s certificate No. 1593627 “A method of determining the carbon dioxide content in alveolar air”

Issued to Konstantin Pavlovich Buteyko. Priority of the invention: 17 October 1986. Registered on 22 May 1990.

Claim of the invention

A method of determining the carbon dioxide content in alveolar air by examining the indicators of the respiratory system, characterised in that, in order to simplify the procedure while keeping it accurate, after 5–10 minutes of rest the patient’s maximum breath-holding time after exhalation is measured with a stopwatch, and the carbon dioxide content is then determined by the formula

P=P 0 +KT,

where P is the percentage of CO2 in alveolar air; P0 = 3.5, the minimum percentage of CO2 in alveolar air; K = 0.05, the coefficient of dependence of the CO2 percentage on the breath-holding time; T is the maximum breath-holding time.

Order No. 591 of the USSR Ministry of Health, 30 April 1985

The order instructed institutes to continue studying the method and to introduce it in the treatment of bronchial asthma. It refers to combined therapy together with medicines and physiotherapy.

Text of the order

MINISTRY OF HEALTH OF THE USSR. ORDER No. 591 of 30 April 1985, Moscow. ON MEASURES TO INTRODUCE THE METHOD OF VOLUNTARY REGULATION OF THE DEPTH OF BREATHING IN THE TREATMENT OF BRONCHIAL ASTHMA

In recent years, non-drug therapy methods have come into wider use in the treatment of patients with bronchial asthma. Studies carried out by a number of research institutes have established the effectiveness of a modified method of voluntary reduction of the depth of breathing (K. P. Buteyko’s author’s certificate for the invention No. 1067640 of 15 September 1983, “A method of treating hemohypocarbia”) in the treatment of bronchial asthma in children and adults as part of combined therapy with drug and physiotherapeutic methods.

In order to further develop non-drug methods of treating bronchial asthma and to introduce the method of voluntary regulation of the depth of breathing in its treatment, I order:

1. The I. M. Sechenov First Moscow Medical Institute of the USSR Ministry of Health (Comrade V. I. Petrov) to continue studying the method of voluntary reduction of the depth of breathing in the treatment of children and adolescents with bronchial asthma, to draw up an instruction for physicians and to submit it to the USSR Ministry of Health for consideration in the established manner by 1 December 1985.

2. The Central Tuberculosis Research Institute of the USSR Ministry of Health (Comrade A. G. Khomenko), the All-Union Research Institute of Pulmonology of the USSR Ministry of Health (Comrade N. M. Putov) and the Moscow Tuberculosis Research Institute of the RSFSR Ministry of Health (Comrade A. A. Priymak) to study during 1985 the method of voluntary reduction of the depth of breathing in the treatment of adult patients with bronchial asthma, to draw up methodological recommendations and to submit them to the USSR Ministry of Health for consideration in the established manner by 1 January 1986.

3. The Institute of Physiology and Pathology of Respiration of the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Medical Sciences (Comrade M. T. Lutsenko), the Institute of Clinical and Experimental Medicine of the same Branch (Comrade V. P. Kaznacheev) and the Institute of Therapy of the same Branch (Comrade Yu. P. Nikitin) to study during 1985–1986 the method of voluntary reduction of the depth of breathing in patients with various diseases of the internal organs, to draw up methodological recommendations on the use of this method of treatment and to submit them by 1 December 1987.

4. The Academic Medical Council of the USSR Ministry of Health (Comrade O. K. Gavrilov), together with the Main Directorate of Medical and Preventive Care for Children and Mothers (Comrade I. I. Grebesheva) and the Main Directorate of Medical and Preventive Care of the USSR Ministry of Health (Comrade A. M. Moskvichev), to hold in December 1986 a scientific and practical conference “Non-drug methods of treating patients with bronchial asthma”.

5. The Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Medical Sciences (Comrade Yu. I. Borodin) to submit to the RSFSR Ministry of Health by 15 June 1985 an application for additional funding to set up a research group acting as a scientific and methodological centre for further study of the method of voluntary reduction of the depth of breathing and its use in various diseases.

6. The I. M. Sechenov First Moscow Medical Institute of the USSR Ministry of Health (Comrade V. I. Petrov) to submit to the USSR Ministry of Health by 1 June 1985 an application for additional funding for the Department of Therapeutic Physical Training (Professor V. A. Siluyanova) to carry out research for further study of the method of voluntary reduction of the depth of breathing.

7. The Chairman of the Coordination Council of the All-Union scientific and technical program 0.69.08 (Comrade A. G. Khomenko) to include in the program by 1 June 1985 additional topics for the 12th five-year plan on research into non-drug methods of treating patients with bronchial asthma using the method of voluntary reduction of the depth of breathing.

8. Control over the execution of this order is entrusted to the Academic Medical Council of the USSR Ministry of Health (Comrade O. K. Gavrilov), the Main Directorate of Medical and Preventive Care for Children and Mothers (Comrade I. I. Grebesheva) and the Main Directorate of Medical and Preventive Care of the USSR Ministry of Health (Comrade A. M. Moskvichev).

Minister S. Burenkov

Report on testing the method in people affected by the Chernobyl nuclear accident, 1990

All-Union Research Centre for Radiation Medicine of the USSR Academy of Medical Sciences, 50 patients. The report describes no control group, so its conclusions remain observations of that time and do not prove an effect.

About medicines. The report speaks of reducing and stopping drug therapy. You must not do this on your own: only your treating doctor makes such decisions.

Text of the report

ORDER OF LENIN ACADEMY OF MEDICAL SCIENCES OF THE USSR, ALL-UNION RESEARCH CENTRE FOR RADIATION MEDICINE. REPORT on testing the VEDB method according to K. P. Buteyko under the agreement of 3 January 1990 with K. P. Buteyko, Candidate of Medical Sciences, on cooperation between the Centre and the health and treatment centre “Breathing according to Buteyko” for 1990

Treatment by the VEDB method was given to 50 patients in the departments of the Institute of Clinical Radiology of the Centre and in the radiation pathology department of the Shevchenko Central District Hospital in Kyiv, in order to study the clinical and pathophysiological mechanisms of Buteyko breathing in people exposed to ionising radiation due to the accident at the Chernobyl nuclear power plant.

The following results were obtained: in 82% of the treated patients, haemodynamic indicators, blood tests and the condition of the digestive system improved. Based on the study of case histories and patient diaries, the following conclusions can be drawn:

1. The use of the VEDB method makes it possible to reduce the amount of medicines used and, in some cases, to stop drug therapy completely.

2. The VEDB method, as part of combined treatment, helps improve blood indicators and some haemodynamic indicators: blood pressure and pulse.

3. The VEDB method, as one of the general strengthening methods of non-traditional medicine, causes no complications or side effects.

4. The VEDB method should in future be recommended, as part of non-drug treatment of people affected by the Chernobyl accident, as one of the methods of rehabilitation.

Director of the Institute of Clinical Radiology, Professor V. G. Bebeshko

Senior Researcher, Department of Neurology, A. B. Denisyuk
