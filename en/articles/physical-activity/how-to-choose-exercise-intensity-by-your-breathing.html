How to choose exercise intensity by your breathing

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. There is no single breathing figure that suits everyone. Take into account your sensations, tolerance and medical restrictions; if you have to hold back your breathing by force, make the load lighter.

Breathing helps you notice how hard an activity has become. But it does not give a single figure by which a load can be prescribed to all people. The practical choice rests on a combination of sensations, the type of movement, medical restrictions and how well rest is tolerated.

Start with a manageable level

For a walk, choose a pace that does not immediately require you to muster your strength. Walk a familiar stretch and assess how freely you move. On another day the same route may feel different, so yesterday’s result does not become an obligation.

If you have to speed up and hold back your breathing by force at the same time, the task is too difficult for such practice. First make the load lighter. There is no need to keep up a speed just because it is shown in an app or suits your companion.

The talk test

CDC offers a simple guide for aerobic activity: at moderate intensity a person can usually talk, but singing is already difficult; at high intensity they can say only a few words between breaths. This is an approximate estimate of effort, not a mandatory goal for a beginner.

You do not need to talk for the whole walk to check. It is enough to understand whether speaking has become noticeably difficult because of the exertion. The test does not measure CO₂, does not determine the control pause and does not replace individual recommendations.

Why additional signs matter

Being able to say a sentence does not rule out all problems. Take into account unusual weakness, dizziness, pain, wheezing and a change in your habitual tolerance. If your condition noticeably worsens, do not try to prove to yourself that the session is safe with the talk test alone.

In the school’s practice, nasal breathing is also considered together with comfort. If it has to be maintained with strained endurance, reduce the load. An indicator should help to choose conditions, not become an exam.

How to discuss increasing the load

It is useful to write down the route or exercise, the approximate duration and your sensations. When the usual option is comfortably tolerated, the next change can be discussed. It is not necessary to change speed, duration and difficulty at once: that makes it harder to understand the cause of the result.

With chronic illnesses, the permissible intensity is agreed with a doctor. The instructor helps to adapt the breathing practice within these limits.
