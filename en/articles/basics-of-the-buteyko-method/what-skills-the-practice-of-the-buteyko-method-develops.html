What skills the practice of the Buteyko method develops

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. The practice teaches you to notice your breathing, to relax, to reduce unnecessary breathing effort without excessive load and to apply these skills in everyday life.

The practice develops not the ability to set a breath-holding record but a set of skills of self-observation and normalization of breathing.

The skill of noticing the features of your breathing

A person learns to recognize a deepening inhalation, a change in rate, unnecessary movement and muscular effort. Over time, they notice better how breathing is affected by stress, emotions, posture, exertion and other factors that deepen breathing.

The skill of relaxing the respiratory muscles

Hearing the advice “relax” is not enough. You need to learn to find tension and remove it without losing a stable body position. This skill becomes the basis for reducing excessive depth of inhalation.

The skill of reducing unnecessary breathing effort

A person distinguishes normal breathing movement from excessive work. They do not forbid themselves to inhale, but remove the effort that keeps ventilation above the current need.

The skill of distinguishing moderate from excessive load

During manageable practice, a slight, relatively comfortable air shortage is possible. Severe suffocation, pronounced tension, large recovery breaths and worsening of one’s condition indicate excessive load. The ability to stop in time is part of the method, not a weakness.

The skill of restoring calm breathing

After a conversation, movement or an emotional episode, a person learns to notice that breathing has remained excessive, to relax and to return to a calm pattern. This transfer makes the practice applicable outside a training session.

The skill of observing trends without records

The control pause, symptoms and well-being are assessed together. Records help to see the trend but do not turn into a daily chase after seconds. A sustained trend matters more than a chance high value.

The skill of applying the principles of the method in everyday life

A person begins to recognize in advance the situations that deepen breathing and organizes their behavior more consciously. They use relaxation and self-observation during work, rest, movement and emotional events, within the limits of the recommendations they have mastered.

Why a skill requires regular practice

The habitual pattern did not form in a single day, so one training session is not enough. Regular assignments, records and the instructor’s correction help to make the actions precise and then more natural. Mastery shows in the ability to apply the principles correctly without constantly struggling with breathing, not in knowing the terms.

This material is for information and does not replace a doctor’s consultation and treatment. Do not cancel or change medicines and their doses on your own. Chest pain, pronounced difficulty breathing or loss of consciousness require emergency help. Breath holds must not be performed in water, while driving or where fainting is dangerous. Results are not guaranteed.
