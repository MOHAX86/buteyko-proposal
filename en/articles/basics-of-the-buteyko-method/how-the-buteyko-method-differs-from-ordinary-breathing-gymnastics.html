How the Buteyko method differs from ordinary breathing gymnastics

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. The Buteyko method differs in its goal and approach: the excess depth of breathing is reduced to the physiological norm primarily through relaxation, and the skill is transferred into everyday life.

The Buteyko method is often called a breathing exercise system, but this definition is too general. It shows neither the goal of the method nor the principle of working with the breathing habit.

Why “breathing exercises” is a broad concept

This name is given to very different systems: with a set rhythm, movements, active inhalations and exhalations, pauses, or a combination of breathing with physical exercise. Therefore it is incorrect to compare the Buteyko method with all breathing exercise systems at once as if they were a single approach.

The main goal of the Buteyko method

The goal of the method is to normalize the gas constants of the respiratory gases and to reduce excess breathing to the physiological norm. What matters is not the number of cycles performed, but a change in the disturbed breathing pattern.

Increasing breathing and reducing excess ventilation

Some practices deliberately intensify inhalations or exhalations. The Buteyko method is aimed at reducing ventilation when it exceeds current metabolic needs. This does not mean a ban on the natural increase in breathing during physical exertion, and it does not mean reducing it below the norm.

The role of relaxation

Relaxation is the main way to remove the excess depth of inhalation; during a session this produces a slight air shortage. The action should not be based on clenching and crude willful suppression. Therefore outwardly small breathing does not yet prove that a person is performing the method correctly.

The role of breath holds

Breath holds may be used for measurement and in certain elements of the practice, but the method is not limited to them. The control pause serves primarily to observe the state of breathing and is performed without turning into strenuous endurance.

The exercise and the everyday breathing skill

A session creates the conditions for learning, but the task is broader: to notice and reduce unnecessary breathing effort in everyday life, to work with factors that deepen breathing and to preserve the acquired skill outside the exercise.

Monitoring changes over time

Correctness is assessed by a combination of indicators: the control pause, changes in symptoms and general condition. A single record or a vivid sensation does not replace a comprehensive assessment.

What is often mistaken for the Buteyko method

Any breath holds, breathing to a count, very infrequent breaths or enduring a severe air shortage are taken for it. A common error unites these variants: attention shifts from normalization and relaxation to outward technique.

A brief comparison without opposing them

Other breathing exercise systems have different goals and ways of being performed. What distinguishes the Buteyko method is work with excess ventilation, relaxation, monitoring of changes over time and transferring the skill into life. This is a description of differences, not a claim that all other approaches are the same or worse.

This material is for information and does not replace a doctor’s consultation and treatment. Do not cancel or change medicines and their doses on your own. Chest pain, pronounced difficulty breathing or loss of consciousness require emergency help. Breath holds must not be performed in water, while driving or where fainting is dangerous.
