The Buteyko method after 1991

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. After 1991, in-person teaching and instructor training continued, international contacts and access to materials expanded, and later remote sessions were added.

After 1991, the history of the Buteyko method continued under new conditions.

What became important for the development of the method was combining two tasks: carefully passing on the content of the training and making it understandable to people from different countries and professional backgrounds.

Materials became more accessible

By the start of the new period, there were lecture recordings, methodological texts, and a book on the experience of introducing the method into medical practice. They helped people get to know the approach beyond a personal meeting with its author.

Printed publications were supplemented by video recordings and later by digital materials. This widened the circle of readers and gave teachers the opportunity to return to the original explanations.

Students’ memoirs made a great contribution to preserving the historical memory. They retain descriptions of classes, discussions, conferences, and everyday work alongside Buteyko.

The international direction

During the 1990s, the method developed in the English-speaking world. Courses were held, teachers were trained, and professional associations arose. The Australian branch, which began in 1990, became one of the notable centers of this spread.

In the United Kingdom, teaching initiatives were active in the second half of the decade, and in 1999 the Buteyko Breathing Association was founded. Acquaintance with the method was increasingly followed by specialist training and research programs.

Passing on the legacy

Buteyko continued to take part in the life of the method and to meet with followers. After his death in 2003, preserving the author’s materials and the quality of teacher training became especially important.

For a student, continuity shows itself in the content of the sessions: whether they understand the goal of the method, are mastering relaxation, can observe their condition, and receive timely feedback.

Modern learning opportunities

Today, remote sessions have been added to books and in-person meetings. They make it possible to discuss the practice, ask questions, and stay regular wherever you live.

At Alik Mullakhmetov’s Health Academy, training is built around normalizing breathing, working with factors that deepen breathing, and observing progress. The ways of passing on knowledge change, while attentive work with the student keeps its importance.
