The Buteyko method and scientific research: an overview

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. The method has been studied in the greatest detail in bronchial asthma: positive results were obtained for symptoms, disease control and use of medicines.

The Buteyko method has a research base that brings together clinical trials, the physiology of breathing and many years of methodological work. These areas help to understand both the practical benefit of training and the role of the respiratory gases in the body.

Physiological research reveals the importance of the respiratory gases: the role of CO₂ in regulating circulation, the acid-base balance and the release of oxygen to the tissues.

What clinical studies have shown

In randomized studies, participants who learned Buteyko breathing reported fewer asthma symptoms and used bronchodilators less often. Some studies recorded a reduction in the dose of inhaled corticosteroids under medical supervision.

For a person, these are concrete results: breathing troubles them less, daily tasks are easier to cope with, and there is more confidence in managing their condition. Such changes make breathing training a significant part of care in asthma.

The studies also included the control pause, breathing discomfort and quality-of-life measures. In modern trials, an increase in the control pause and an improvement in asthma control were observed after training. Changes in spirometry were less consistent, so the main clinical advantage of the method is associated primarily with symptoms and control of the condition.

Why physiology matters

Breathing provides the intake of oxygen and the removal of carbon dioxide. The CO₂ level is linked to the volume of ventilation relative to metabolism. Carbon dioxide itself takes part in regulating vascular tone and the conditions under which hemoglobin releases oxygen to the tissues.

This physiological basis explains why paying attention to excess ventilation makes sense. In the breathing school of Alik Mullakhmetov’s Health Academy, the aim of the method is formulated as the normalization of breathing and gas parameters, mainly through relaxation and work with factors that deepen breathing.

What the reader gets

Training with an instructor helps to organize the practice correctly. If you have a disease, the exercises are coordinated with medical management; any change in drug therapy is discussed with a doctor.
