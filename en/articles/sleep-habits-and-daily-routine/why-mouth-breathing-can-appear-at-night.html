Why mouth breathing can appear at night

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. The cause may be a blocked nose, habit, position or features of the airways. First the cause is established, and snoring, pauses in breathing, choking and severe daytime sleepiness need to be discussed with a doctor.

Mouth open during sleep at night: what it means

A person may notice a dry mouth in the morning or hear from a family member that they slept with their mouth open. This is an observation, not a diagnosis and not a direct measurement of the amount of air. The causes vary: a blocked nose, habit, body position, features of the upper airways. A single morning sensation cannot show how long a person breathed through the mouth during sleep.

Start with how clear your nose is

If your nose breathes poorly in the evening or at night, it is unwise to keep your lips closed by force. Find out whether the congestion recurs and whether it is connected with a cold, an allergy or the environment. If the problem persists, see a doctor. Work on the daytime habit of nasal breathing is possible when the nose is clear and the person does not feel marked discomfort.

Snoring and possible apnea

Snoring does not always mean sleep apnea. But breathing stops noticed by family members, sudden awakenings with choking and severe daytime sleepiness require examination. A doctor will determine whether sleep studies are needed and which treatment is suitable.

How to observe without self-diagnosis

For several days, note what time you went to bed, how freely your nose breathed before sleep, and whether there were awakenings and dryness in the morning. It is useful to write down whether the surroundings changed or any medicines were taken. Do not draw conclusions from one night. Daytime Buteyko exercises are chosen with an instructor; the task is not to consciously control your breathing throughout sleep.

Why you cannot simply tape your mouth shut

With a blocked nose or an unclarified cause of night symptoms, you should not tape your mouth on your own or keep it closed with bandages. First you need to assess how open the airways are and find out the cause of the complaints.

What to tell a doctor or an instructor

It is useful to tell the doctor how long ago the dry mouth and snoring appeared, whether your nose gets blocked and whether anyone has noticed breathing stops. Tell the instructor how you breathe through your nose during the day and what difficulties arise with the prescribed exercises. Specific observations are more useful than the assumption that you breathe wrongly all night.

When an in-person doctor’s consultation is needed

This material is for information and does not replace a doctor’s consultation and treatment. Do not cancel or change medicines and their doses on your own. Chest pain, pronounced difficulty breathing or loss of consciousness require emergency help. Breath holds must not be performed in water, while driving or where fainting is dangerous.
