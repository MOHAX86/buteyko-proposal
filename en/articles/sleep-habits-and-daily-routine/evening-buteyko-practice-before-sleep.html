Evening Buteyko practice before sleep

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. The school holds that evening practice helps prepare breathing for the night’s rest. Light exercise, relaxation or a break is chosen together with an instructor according to the person’s condition.

The task before sleep

In the methodological position of the school, evening practice helps prepare breathing for the night’s rest. The goal is to normalize blood gas values, primarily CO₂, and to reduce excessive depth of breathing to the physiological norm. The exercise is chosen according to the person’s condition and the skills already mastered.

Why there is no single scenario

Before sleep, people differ in how they feel and in the state of their autonomic nervous system. For one person, light physical exercise within an agreed program is suitable; for another, relaxation and calm breathing practice are more useful that same evening. Sometimes the load needs to be reduced or a break taken. The instructor takes the person’s response into account instead of prescribing everyone the same sequence by the clock.

The option with movement

If the individual program includes manageable physical exercise, it remains precisely light: without chasing intensity, marked shortness of breath or overtiring before sleep. Breathing during movement naturally changes in line with the work of the muscles.

The option with relaxation

When there is a lot of tension or little energy, the instructor may choose calmer work: a comfortable position, relaxation of the face, shoulders and belly, observing the breathing without forcing it. In the school, excessive depth of breathing is reduced primarily through relaxation. There is no need to hold back the inhalation by force, push for a strong air shortage or repeat measurements until the figure improves.

How to assess the response

After the agreed practice, one looks at the general condition, symptoms and, where appropriate, the trend of the control pause under comparable conditions. If marked discomfort, dizziness or worsening appears, the exercise is stopped and discussed with an instructor.

What matters for sleep besides the exercise

A regular sleep time, a reasonably quiet and comfortable room, moderate caffeine intake and treatment of illnesses remain important. If you snore and apnea is suspected, or you wake up choking, see a doctor. Breathing practice does not replace examination and prescribed therapy.
