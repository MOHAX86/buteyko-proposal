How not to turn a breathing exercise into a struggle with yourself

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. Do not try to achieve a result by force: relax, do not compete in breath holds and stop the session if there is marked discomfort.

Where the strain comes from

The wish to get a result as soon as possible sometimes makes a person hold their breath, watch the stopwatch and resist a natural inhalation. In this mode, even the word “relaxation” turns into a new task that has to be carried out by force.

Will in the VEDB method

According to the school’s methodological base, voluntary effort is the organization of regular work to normalize breathing. The excess depth of inhalation is reduced mainly through relaxation and only to the physiological norm; the air shortage remains slight. There is no need to compete in the length of breath holds or to deliberately endure suffocation.

When to stop

Marked discomfort, intensified symptoms, worsening well-being or a strong desire to compensate for the exercise with a big breath are a reason to stop the session. The next step is to rest and tell your instructor exactly what happened.

How to return to the task

Check again that your position is comfortable and check for tension in your face, shoulders and abdomen. Do not rush to repeat the attempt immediately.

This material is for information and does not replace a doctor’s consultation and treatment. Do not cancel or change medicines and their doses on your own. Chest pain, pronounced difficulty breathing or loss of consciousness require emergency help. Breath holds must not be performed in water, while driving or where fainting is dangerous.
