Gentle practice when you are tired

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. When tired, reduce the load: give yourself rest and a comfortable posture, do not force the inhalation and do not chase your previous numbers.

First assess your condition

After a hard day, a person’s capacities differ from those in the morning. Fatigue is a signal to reduce the load, not a reason to demand of yourself your previous control pause readings. If the weakness is unusual, marked or does not go away, it is important to discuss it with a doctor.

What gentleness means

Provide a comfortable position and time to rest. Observe your face, shoulders and natural breathing; do not force the inhalation and do not try to “make up for” a missed session with a long breath hold. Even a brief observation of excess tension can be a meaningful part of the work.

Checking the result

The school assesses not only the control pause number, but also symptoms and general well-being. If you feel worse after an exercise, it should be stopped and corrected with an instructor. When you are tired, it is especially important not to turn practice into a fight with yourself.

Next

The specific duration and amount of practice are determined individually, taking the person’s condition into account. If after a working day you feel tiredness and heaviness in your body, read about when to choose a relaxing massage.
