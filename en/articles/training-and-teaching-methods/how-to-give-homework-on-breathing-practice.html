How to give homework on breathing practice

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. Homework should be clear, manageable and gone through with the instructor in advance: a clear learning goal, agreed records and discussion at the next meeting.

Homework reinforces a specific skill and provides material for the next meeting.

One clear learning goal

Instead of a long list of actions, it is useful to state what the assignment is about. For example, learning to notice tension during practice that has already been mastered, or keeping records of what was done. The amount and conditions of the work are agreed separately, taking the student’s preparation into account.

Before the lesson ends, the teacher asks the person to repeat the assignment in their own words. It is necessary to make sure that the action itself, the agreed amount, the records and the way to ask a question are all clear. A written version helps avoid a situation where the person remembers the explanation differently at home.

What to write down

A diary should help with the review rather than turn into an additional ordeal. In it you can note what was done, the circumstances, sensations and questions. If measurements are planned, it is important to keep the agreed conditions and not to add many repetitions for the sake of a better number.

An example of a learning task is to perform a familiar action at an agreed time and note whether it was possible to manage without unnecessary tension. The specific duration and technique are determined by the instructor. Such an example shows how an assignment is structured, but does not replace an individual program.

How to discuss an assignment that was not done

A missed assignment is a reason to find out why. Perhaps the task turned out to be too difficult, did not fit into the schedule or was misunderstood. Reproaches do not remove these obstacles. It is more useful to change the wording, the amount or the organization of the work.

Result

Good homework continues in-person or remote training and always comes back into the discussion. New complex actions are first mastered with the teacher and then consolidated independently.
