How to track a student’s progress

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. Progress is assessed by how the student understands the method, carries out assignments and applies skills. Along with this, the school looks at control pause trends, symptoms and general well-being.

Quality of the skill

A person may memorize an explanation but perform the action differently at home. It is therefore important to periodically ask them to show their usual practice without any prompts beforehand. This reveals what has taken hold and what needs to be revisited.

Signs of progress in learning are that the student describes sensations more precisely, notices unnecessary tension, understands the assignment and asks specific questions. The ability to report a difficulty in time is also important: it helps the teacher adjust the training.

Diary and comparable observations

Records make it possible to discuss not the impression from the last day but a sequence of sessions. It is useful to take into account the measurement conditions and the circumstances of the practice. One-off figures without context can be misleading.

The school’s methodological base describes the changes expected after correctly performed practice. These benchmarks should not be turned into a mandatory result of every session or into grounds for demanding a required number from the student. If the expected change is absent, the performance and circumstances are examined, instead of proposing to raise the indicator at any cost.

What to discuss at a meeting

It is convenient to go through three questions in order: what was done, what has changed and what remains difficult. Then the next learning task is defined. Sometimes a sensible next step is to repeat the basics and simplify the assignment in order to eliminate a persistent mistake.

Result

Tracking progress helps to choose further actions. Its purpose is to see real changes and difficulties, not to build a mandatory graph of rising indicators.
