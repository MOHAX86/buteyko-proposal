How the first session in the Buteyko method goes

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. At the first lesson, the instructor talks with the person, explains the basics and checks the initial action. The purpose of the meeting is to lay a foundation, not to give all the exercises at once.

The first lesson introduces the person to the principles of the method and lets the instructor see how they understand and perform the initial actions.

Conversation and explanation

In the methodological base of Alik Mullakhmetov’s school, training begins with gathering information and a conversation. The person’s request, previous experience, features of their well-being and the circumstances in which breathing deepens are discussed. It is important to talk about current treatment and any difficulties, rather than trying to appear better prepared.

The teacher then introduces the student to the basic concepts. They explain that the goal of the work is to normalize breathing, and that its excessive depth is reduced mainly through relaxation. The control pause is introduced as a separate measurement that should not be turned into a competition.

First practical actions

The instructor helps to choose a comfortable position, shows the initial action and watches the student’s attempt. Afterwards, sensations are discussed: what worked, where tension arose and which part of the explanation remained unclear. It is useful to correct a misunderstood principle right away.

For the first meeting, it is useful to prepare questions and notes about previous practice experience. There is no need to try in advance to master complex techniques or to show the longest possible breath hold. It is more important for the teacher to see your habitual performance and understand which explanation you need.

What should be clear by the end of the meeting

The student should know which action has been suggested to them, how to do the agreed homework, what to write down and how to contact the instructor. If the explanation remains unclear, it is better to sort it out before practicing independently.

Result

The specific content of a lesson depends on the student’s initial preparation and the teacher’s work.
