How to choose a teacher of the Buteyko method

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. Find out where the teacher trained, how they organize lessons and how they check performance. Persuasive speech and a popular channel do not yet show an ability to teach.

What to ask before starting lessons

Ask the teacher to tell you about their training and practical experience. Find out what the course is about, how the meetings are run, whether skills are checked and how to ask questions between lessons. If a training document is presented, it is useful to understand who issued it and what training exactly it confirms.

Pay attention to the explanations. A teacher should be able to explain the basic concepts in plain language without substituting complicated words for an answer. A good sign is a willingness to clarify your question, show the action and check whether you have understood it correctly.

How to assess the approach to the student

People differ in their initial skills, experience and abilities. It is therefore important that the teacher takes an interest in your situation and adjusts assignments based on observation. Find out in advance what to do if an assignment is unclear or if doing it causes pronounced discomfort.

Reviews can help you form an idea of how the course communicates and how it is organized. Still, someone else’s result does not become a promise of the same result for you. It is more useful to look for specific descriptions: whether the teacher checked performance, answered questions and helped to sort out mistakes.

The materials of Alik Mullakhmetov’s school for training instructors give a CP benchmark of 40 seconds. This is an internal methodological criterion; assessing a teacher by a single indicator is not enough. Accuracy of terminology, experience, a respectful manner of communication and the ability to teach are also important.

Result

Choose a teacher based on a combination of features: training, a clear program, personal feedback and an attentive attitude to performance. The terms of study should be clear before payment.
