Dizziness during breathing exercises: possible causes

Author: Alik Mullakhmetov, VEDB senior instructor and examiner, more than 30 years of practicing the Buteyko method

Updated: November 25, 2026

In short. If you feel dizzy, stop the exercise and sit or lie down in a safe place. A recurring or unusual symptom needs to be discussed with a doctor.

Dizziness can occur for various reasons. It should not be considered a compulsory stage of mastering the method or a sign that the load needs to be increased.

What happened before the symptom

To begin with, it is useful to recall the specific action: the person was trying to inhale less, was holding their breath, was sighing often or abruptly changed body position. It is also important whether such a sensation has occurred before outside of sessions.

In the school’s teaching materials, dizziness is mentioned among the possible manifestations of increased ventilation. But this does not mean that every case is explained by breathing alone. Other conditions may also play a part, including changes in blood pressure, inner ear diseases and the effects of medicines.

What to do right away

Stop the task, sit or lie down in a safe place and avoid getting up abruptly. You should not walk on stairs, get behind the wheel or continue the exercise standing while the unsteadiness persists.

There is no need to carry out new breathing tests to confirm the presumed cause. First it is important to regain stability and assess how you feel.

When to discuss it with a doctor

Recurring, prolonged or unusual dizziness requires medical assessment. The doctor should be told about accompanying changes in hearing, vision or pulse and about fainting episodes.

Sudden weakness on one side of the body, impaired speech, a severe new headache, chest pain or loss of consciousness require emergency help.

How to change further practice

The instructor is told how the exercise was performed, how long it lasted and the sequence of sensations. After that it is possible to check whether there was excessive effort or a misunderstood instruction. If necessary, the return to sessions is postponed until a medical consultation.

Such a review helps understand the cause and choose a task. There is no need to continue the previous load through dizziness in order to get used to it.

When an in-person doctor’s consultation is needed

This material is for information and does not replace a doctor’s consultation and treatment. Do not cancel or change medicines and their doses on your own. Chest pain, pronounced difficulty breathing or loss of consciousness require emergency help. Breath holds must not be performed in water, while driving or where fainting is dangerous.
