Who it is for

Questions

Questions about the training

Alik will discuss other questions with you personally.

Is medical education required?

No. It is enough to be at least 18, study regularly and have your own practice.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, payment in installments is agreed individually. Payment by a Ukrainian bank card or by bank details for foreign cards.

Is the final exam difficult?

For students who regularly completed the assignments, it is usually not difficult. You may use your notes.

What language is the diploma in?

In Ukrainian, English or another language on request. It has no expiry date.
