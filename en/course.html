One-to-one training in the Buteyko method

In short. The Buteyko method course is 10 one-to-one sessions of 30–60 minutes, 2–3 times a week, over one month, in Zoom, Telegram, or another convenient messenger. Between sessions there are home assignments and regular contact in a chat.

The course costs 450 € (or the equivalent at your bank’s exchange rate). The preliminary consultation is free.

An individual program of training in the Buteyko method with personal support, practical sessions, and the gradual formation of a physiological breathing pattern

How the training works

Training takes place one-to-one in Zoom, Telegram, or another convenient messenger.

The course includes 10 one-to-one sessions lasting from 30 to 60 minutes. Sessions are held 2–3 times a week, and the full course must be completed within one month.

During the sessions we study and practice exercises of the Buteyko method. Between meetings, the participant does home assignments and repeats the mastered exercises independently.

The following are monitored during the training:

  • the control pause and its trend;
  • pulse rate;
  • the skill of reducing ventilation and managing breathing;
  • correct performance of the exercises;
  • understanding of the principles of the method;
  • changes in well-being and the trend of symptoms.

Between sessions, regular contact is maintained in a chat. The participant sends short reports on their practice, and I check them and adjust the work if necessary. Text and voice messages can be used and, if needed, a short video call.

The program has a general structure, but the specific exercises, the intensity of practice, and the pace of training are chosen individually.

First session

The first session begins with getting acquainted and assessing the initial condition. We discuss well-being, lifestyle, past illnesses, individual features, and the problems with which the person has come to the course. Initial measurements are taken, in particular of the control pause and pulse rate, and the features of breathing are assessed. Then I explain the basic principles of the Buteyko method and lead the first practice. Already at the first session, the participant begins to master the skill of reducing ventilation and of more physiological breathing.

Safety questions are also discussed before the course begins. The Buteyko method does not replace medical diagnosis and treatment. We do not prescribe or cancel medicines and do not change a doctor’s prescriptions. If there are illnesses, the participant stays in contact with their doctor. If necessary, participation in breathing practices should be agreed with a doctor in advance.

Course materials

During the training, the participant receives additional materials that help to understand the method better and to practice independently between sessions. The materials are given for good, for unlimited use.
The course materials may include:

  • video lessons and recordings of practical sessions;
  • tables for tracking your figures;
  • methodological materials;
  • books and further reading;
  • descriptions of exercises;
  • materials on the physiology of breathing;
  • examples and the experience of other participants.

The materials are used as an addition to individual work and help to form the skill of independent practice.

Outcome of the training

The main goal of the course is to teach a person to use the Buteyko method independently.

After completing the training, the participant should understand the principles of the method, be able to perform the basic exercises correctly, control their breathing, and independently enter the practice of reducing ventilation. A person learns to apply the method to normalize the breathing pattern, improve general well-being and, where possible, reduce the severity of certain functional symptoms.
Particular attention is paid to ensuring that after the course the participant does not remain constantly dependent on the teacher, but can continue practicing independently, competently and safely.

Support during and after the course

Throughout the individual course, the participant is regularly in contact with me between sessions.

You can send short reports, questions, and text and voice messages in the chat. If necessary, an additional short video call is possible. While taking the individual course, the participant also gets free access to the regular group practice sessions and educational programs held during this period.
After the ten main sessions are completed, if necessary, I continue the support free of charge for another 30 days. During this period you can ask questions, send the results of your practice, and receive recommendations on further independent work.

Important information about the method and safety

I teach the Buteyko method as a system of breathing practices that can be applied within health practice and physical therapy.
During the training we work with the breathing pattern, the skill of reducing excess ventilation, and the independent performance of breathing exercises.
The Buteyko method does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment prescribed by a doctor.
We do not prescribe or cancel medicines and do not change a doctor’s prescriptions. If you have chronic illnesses or other conditions, participation in breathing practices should be agreed with your doctor.

Training program

The program consists of a theoretical part and a practical part. Theory gives an understanding of the physiological mechanisms of breathing and the principles of the Buteyko method, and the practical part forms the skill of working with your breathing independently.

Theoretical part

  • basic principles of the Buteyko method;
  • physiology of breathing and gas exchange;
  • the role of carbon dioxide and oxygen in the regulation of breathing;
  • the concept of excess lung ventilation;
  • mechanisms by which the breathing pattern is formed;
  • the control pause and its use for assessing breathing;
  • pulse rate and other self-monitoring indicators;
  • the deep breathing test, Buteyko’s hyperventilation test;
  • the connection of breathing with physical load, relaxation, and general well-being;
  • principles of safe independent practice.

Practical part

  • determining the initial indicators and individual features of breathing;
  • mastering the skill of reducing ventilation;
  • forming a more physiological breathing pattern;
  • practices of relaxation and of reducing excess breathing effort;
  • performing the exercises of the Buteyko method at rest;
  • gradual application of the skills during everyday activity and physical load;
  • monitoring the control pause, pulse, well-being, and the trend of symptoms;
  • home practice between sessions;
  • checking technique and correcting exercises individually;
  • gradually making the practice more demanding in line with your condition and preparation;
  • forming the skill of entering the practice independently;
  • preparing to use the method independently after the course is completed.

The program follows a general sequence, but the specific amount of theory, the exercises, their intensity, and the pace of mastering them are chosen individually.
